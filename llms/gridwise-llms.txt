# Gridwise

> Gridwise is an earnings-tracking and optimization platform for rideshare and delivery gig workers (Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Grubhub, Instacart, Amazon Flex), paired with Gridwise Analytics — a B2B mobility-data business that turns millions of anonymized driver location, trip, and earnings records into industry benchmarking, KPI, and market-intelligence products. Gridwise does not currently publish a public developer API; the surfaces below are its consumer app, data product, and public policy/support pages.

## Products
- [Gridwise driver app](https://gridwise.io/): Mileage, earnings, and performance tracking across gig platforms for drivers.
- [Gridwise Analytics](https://gridwise.io/analytics): B2B mobility-data product — Gridwise Reports (aggregated weekly metrics), record-level trip data, and consumer/driver surveys for gig platforms, financial services, retail/merchants, and AV fleets.
- [Gridwise Analytics — contact / demo](https://gridwise.io/analytics/contact): Request a demo or data sample.
